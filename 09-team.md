# 9. Team & Entity

## 9.1 Legal Entity

Aivive operates under **Yu Network SRL**, an international entity structured for neutral, single-jurisdiction token operation. The legal entity is separated from the operating team to maintain clean compliance boundaries between protocol governance, treasury custody, and product development.

## 9.2 Core Team

The core team is intentionally small and capital-efficient. Two senior contributors operate alongside a Claude-based AI development harness — a structure deliberately chosen to maximize shipping velocity while keeping headcount overhead minimal.

| Role | Holder |
|---|---|
| **Founder & CEO** | Mason Reeves |
| **Co-Founder & CTO** | Avery Hart |
| **AI Development Harness** | Claude Opus, operating throughout the build pipeline |

### Mason Reeves — Founder & CEO

Mason previously led product at a Series B AI consumer startup in San Francisco, where he built creator-facing tools at the intersection of design and generative AI. His background spans five years of consumer software at high-growth companies, with a focus on visual creation interfaces and community-driven product loops.

He first encountered crypto in 2018, became a full-time Web3 builder in 2022, and has since spent two years thinking about the structural gap between AI consumer applications and on-chain monetary mechanics — a gap Aivive is the direct answer to.

### Avery Hart — Co-Founder & CTO

Avery is a senior engineer with deep Solana experience, including production work on consumer-facing decentralized applications and high-throughput on-chain payment systems. His background combines a decade in distributed systems engineering with three years of full-time Solana protocol work, including direct experience integrating Wormhole CCTP, Jupiter routing, and Squads multisig — the three primitives at the core of the Aivive economic loop.

Prior to Aivive, Avery served on the engineering team of a leading Solana ecosystem infrastructure project.

### A Note on Team Size

The product surface is narrow. The infrastructure choices are mature. The economic loop is automated. **The investment is in shipping, not in headcount** — and a small, focused team aligned through equity rather than salary is precisely the right structure for an early-stage protocol that needs to ship 60 days from inception.
